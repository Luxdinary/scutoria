# Map of all long videos: 01 to 24

As of 30 October 2026, evening. For Stefan; English counterpart of `KARTE.de.md`. One page for everything: what each video explains, for whom, what it points to on scutoria.com, which product it names, where the end screen leads and how far it is. Basis: `THEMEN.md` (videos 01 to 10, approved scripts in `SKRIPTE/`) and `THEMEN-STAFFEL-2.en.md` (videos 11 to 24, draft).

## Summary

- **24 videos in two seasons.** Season 1 (01 to 10) is written and approved by you, about 12 to 12.5 minutes each in English. Season 2 (11 to 24) is a topic list, 12 to 30 minutes per video.
- **Every video points to scutoria.com.** In the closing section every video names the free offer and then the toolkit with the price sentence. In addition the free offer appears once in the middle, in season 1 as a visual element, in season 2 in the narration as well.
- **Every free offer and both toolkits appear several times.** Only two documents of the Security Evidence Toolkit are never the focus: the clause checklist and the evidence summary.
- **Total length:** season 1 about 2 hours, season 2 about 4 to 5 hours, per language.

## What the map shows

- **Weight of the lines:** season 1 is balanced: five videos blue, four turquoise, plus 03 for both. Season 2 leans clearly towards AI: nine videos end only with the AI Policy Toolkit, two only with the Security Evidence Toolkit (11, 18), two with both (15, 22) and one with neither (14). That follows from your brief on the use of AI.
- **Gap in the video coverage:** the clause checklist and the evidence summary of the Security Evidence Toolkit have no video of their own. `THEMEN.md` already names "clause checklist and DORA" as a later topic.
- **End screens in season 1:** four videos point back instead of forward: 04 and 07 to the walk-through 03, 06 and 10 to the questionnaire video 02. That is how the approved scripts read, and it makes sense as a path to the product. End screens for season 2 are not set yet; proposal: each video points to the next and to 13 or 03 as the walk-through.
- **Pointer in the middle for season 1:** decided on 30 October ("Ja, einbauen"): in the animated version the free offer also appears once in the middle of videos 01 to 10 as a visual element, where the video talks about it. The narration stays unchanged. Position, word anchor and path per video: `ANIMATION/VORLAGE-PARALLEL.md`, section 3a.
- **Private individuals:** six videos speak to private individuals (11, 12, 14, 18, 20, 21), plus 16 to employees and 23 to the self-employed. Only 14 has no product; the video says so openly.
