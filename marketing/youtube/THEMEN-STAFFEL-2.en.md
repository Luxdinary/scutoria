# Topics, season 2: AI with rules, at home and at work

As of 30 October 2026. Draft for Stefan; English counterpart of `THEMEN-STAFFEL-2.de.md`. Fourteen long videos of 12 to 30 minutes, numbered 11 to 24 as a continuation of the ten approved scripts (`THEMEN.md`). The number is the recommended publishing order.

## Summary

- **Fourteen topics:** eight speak to private individuals, employees or the self-employed (five of them to businesses as well), six to businesses only. No topic repeats videos 01 to 10; where one builds on them, it says so under "Boundary".
- **A pointer to the website in every video, twice:** in the middle a free tool, memo or sample from scutoria.com that fits the content; at the end the matching product. Both with the path on screen and a link in the description.
- **Honest product fit:** the toolkits are for businesses. Purely private topics name only the free tool and say openly that there is no product for private individuals.
- **Recommendation:** start with video 11 (fraud with AI voices). It speaks to private individuals and businesses at once and leads to the Security Evidence Toolkit.

## Rules for this season

- **Wording:** the brief says "safe use of AI". Titles and narration never use "safe" or "secure" as a promise, as the fact sheet requires (`VORLAGEN/FAKTEN-PRODUKTE.md`, sections 1 and 10). The titles therefore say "check", "release", "with rules".
- **Structure:** every video opens with a question; the spoken notice "This is Scutoria … no legal advice" comes at the end, the on-screen line "Synthetic voice" stays from second 0 (your decision of 1 November 2026, "Ja, für alle"; `KENNZEICHNUNG.md`).
- **Sources:** every deadline and legal statement in this list comes from the fact sheet and carries its source with retrieval date, abbreviated "FS no.". Anything new is listed under "To be sourced". It is read at the original and added to the fact sheet before the script is written; until then it is marked "check status".
- **Length:** the minutes are estimates from the number of chapters. The English voice speaks about 160 words per minute (video 01: 1,994 words in 12.5 minutes, `SKRIPTE/01-ai-rules-start.en.md`). So 12 minutes are about 1,900 words, 30 minutes about 4,800.
- **Approved sentences:** pointers to memos, tools and prices only in the wording of the fact sheet, sections 5 to 7. The price only as: "The toolkit costs 199 euros per year. The first ten buyers pay 99."

## 11 · Fake voices, fake bosses. How to spot fraud with AI.

- **Audience:** private individuals and businesses. **Length:** 20 to 25 minutes.
- **Benefit:** the viewer recognises the patterns (a familiar voice on the phone, a video call, changed bank details), has two fixed counter-rules (call back on a known number, a second person approves) and knows where to report.
- **Chapters:**
  1. What a deep fake is: AI-generated or manipulated image, audio or video content that resembles real persons and would falsely appear authentic (FS 12: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Art. 3(60) · retrieved 2026-10-23).
  2. At home: the call in a relative's voice. The family rule: hang up, call back, agreed check question.
  3. At work: the boss's call, the supplier's email, the new account number. The Austrian Federal Criminal Police Office recommends the four-eyes principle, calling back on known numbers, and never changing bank details on the basis of an email alone (FS 28: Bundeskriminalamt, CEO Fraud / Business E-Mail Compromise, updated 29 June 2026 · retrieved 2026-10-19).
  4. What your own voice and photos online mean (only with a new source, see below).
  5. When it has happened: emergency numbers and reporting points in Austria (FS 24 · retrieved 2026-10-19 and 2026-10-23) and Germany (FS 25 · retrieved 2026-10-19 and 2026-10-23).
  6. The rule on one card, for the wall at home and at work.
- **To be sourced:** police warnings about shock calls with AI voices (Austria and Germany), recommendation of an agreed check word; no case numbers without a source. Check status.
- **Pointer in the middle:** the public sample emergency card of the example business on scutoria.com (Security Evidence Toolkit, D3).
- **Product at the end:** Security Evidence Toolkit: D3 emergency card (the first hour, whom to call, the deadlines), D2 action plan. Fit: strong for businesses. Private: no product; the family rule is in the video itself.
- **Boundary:** video 08 covers the first hour after an incident; this video the deception before it.
- **Search terms:** ai voice scam, deepfake phone call how to spot, ai voice cloning fraud, ceo fraud deepfake, changed bank details scam.

## 14 · Your data and other people's. What stays out of AI chats at home.

- **Audience:** private individuals. **Length:** 15 to 18 minutes.
- **Benefit:** the viewer can name five kinds of information they do not type even privately: health, access data, ID and bank details, information about other people (family, children, friends), confidential documents. They know the placeholder rule.
- **Chapters:**
  1. Why private does not mean it does not matter: you also pass on information about others (interpretation).
  2. Health and medical findings.
  3. Access data, ID, bank account.
  4. Photos and documents of others, especially of children.
  5. Placeholders: [name], [place], [date].
  6. Checklist.
- **Legal frame, honestly:** the GDPR does not apply to purely personal activities; the provider, however, processes the data. The video therefore argues with consequences, not duties. Health data are a special category for businesses (FS 10: Regulation (EU) 2016/679, Art. 9(1) · retrieved 2026-10-23); this belongs only in the bridge to work.
- **To be sourced:** GDPR Art. 2(2)(c) (exemption for purely personal or household activities). Check status.
- **Pointer in the middle:** tool "Is there anything personal in it?": marks email addresses, phone numbers, IBANs, names, addresses, health terms and access data and replaces them with placeholders on request. Limit in the approved wording: "Treat green as nothing obvious."
- **Product at the end:** none for private individuals; the video says so. Sentence for employees: for work, video 04 and the one page to post (AI Policy Toolkit, D2) show the rule. Fit: none, with a bridge.
- **Boundary:** video 04 asks the same question for the business.
- **Search terms:** what not to tell chatgpt, health data ai chatbot, children's photos ai, personal data ai privacy, ai placeholders.

## Open points

- **Private individuals as an audience:** the brand addresses businesses without an IT department up to about fifty staff. The private topics therefore always build a bridge to work (employees, the self-employed). Purely private topics without that bridge would change the positioning.
- **New sources:** eight of the fourteen videos need new sources before a script is written (under "To be sourced" in each). This is research by Claude, at no cost.
