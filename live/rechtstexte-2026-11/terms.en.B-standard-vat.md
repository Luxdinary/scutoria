# Terms

## 2. What you buy

Scutoria sells annual licences for digital products ("toolkits"). A licence covers, for twelve months from purchase: the online questionnaire (generator), the documents it produces as PDF, DOCX and XLSX, the accompanying handbook, the templates, the evidence page, the customer account with its evidence folder, and every update to the product during that time. The exact scope is the one shown on the product page at the time of the order.

The products are guides and templates. They are not legal advice, not a certificate and not a confirmation that a business complies with a rule.

## 3. Ordering and conclusion of the contract

The presentation of the products is not a binding offer. "Buy annual licence" takes you to Stripe's payment page; by clicking the payment button there you make a binding offer. The contract is concluded when Scutoria confirms the order by email and provides access. Payment is processed by Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Limited, Dublin). Contract languages are English and German; in case of discrepancy between the language versions the German version prevails.

Access is provided immediately after receipt of payment: by email with a personal link and in the customer account. Delivery is complete once access is provided.

## 6. Right of withdrawal for consumers

Consumers have a fourteen-day right of withdrawal; details are in the withdrawal notice. For digital content it expires if, before the period ends, you expressly agree that delivery starts immediately and confirm that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal (section 18 (1) 11 of the Austrian Distance and Off-Premises Contracts Act, FAGG). Checkout collects that consent; the access email confirms the contract and your consent. You can also withdraw through the “Withdraw from contract” function linked at the bottom of every page. Businesses have no right of withdrawal.

## 7. Licence

The licence covers one business (one company) and its staff. You may adapt the generated documents and templates for that business, use them internally, print them and present them to customers, insurers or authorities. Not permitted: passing on or reselling the products, using them for several businesses, use by consultants for their clients without a separate agreement, and publishing the handbook.

## 8. Term and renewal

The licence runs for twelve months from purchase and ends without notice. It does not renew automatically. Before it expires you receive a reminder; renewal is a new purchase. After expiry the documents produced so far remain available in the account for twelve months and are then deleted; the generator and updates are no longer available after expiry. Download documents you need for longer before then.
