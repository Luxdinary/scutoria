# Withdrawal notice

## Right of withdrawal

Consumers have the right to withdraw from this contract within fourteen days without giving any reason. The withdrawal period is fourteen days from the day the contract is concluded.

To exercise the right of withdrawal, you must inform us, Stefan Dallinger, Kleinpoppen 18, 3902 Vitis, Austria, kontakt@scutoria.com, of your decision to withdraw from this contract by an unequivocal statement (for example an email). You can use the “Withdraw from contract” function linked at the bottom of every page, or the model withdrawal form, which is not obligatory. To meet the deadline it is sufficient that you send your communication before the withdrawal period has expired.

## Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we shall reimburse all payments received from you without undue delay and in any event not later than fourteen days from the day on which we are informed of your decision to withdraw. We will carry out the reimbursement using the same means of payment you used for the initial transaction; you will not incur any fees as a result.

## Expiry of the right of withdrawal for digital content

For contracts for the supply of digital content not supplied on a tangible medium, the right of withdrawal expires if Scutoria has begun delivery before the end of the withdrawal period after you expressly agreed that delivery starts immediately, acknowledged that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal, and we confirmed the contract including that consent to you (section 18 (1) 11 of the Austrian Distance and Off-Premises Contracts Act, FAGG). You give the consent at checkout by ticking the box; the confirmation comes with the email that provides access.
