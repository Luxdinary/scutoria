# How we work

Scutoria makes information security, compliance and the use of AI workable for businesses without an IT department. This page says how the content is made, what it does and where its limits are.

## What Scutoria is for

For businesses with up to about fifty staff, where management decides for itself because there is no IT or security department. Three subjects belong together: security, business and AI.

Two toolkits today and a third in preparation, each built from your answers: an online questionnaire that generates the finished documents, a short handbook that explains every decision with its source, templates, an evidence page, and twelve months of updates. Free tools alongside: a few questions, then a dated memo with the relevant articles.

## How we research

Every statement carries its source: publisher, title, date of the document, retrieved date and link. We read legal texts in the original, for example in the Official Journal of the European Union or the Austrian legal information system, not in third-party summaries.

What cannot be sourced is called "not determinable", with the reason. We do not estimate, round or fill a gap plausibly.

## How we handle uncertainty

Every handbook marks each statement with one of four markers: Fact, stated in the cited source; Interpretation, our reading, marked as such; Uncertain, not settled, with the reason visible; Gap, missing and named, not filled. Every product ends with its sources and its open points. Nothing disappears quietly into the running text.

## How we update

Updates within a licence year appear in your account without you having to do anything; the version line on every page shows the state. Setup guides carry the date of the interface shown, because every interface ages. Tools name the date of their sources on the page.

## How we use tools

The content is produced with AI assistance and checked by people before release: sources re-read, figures and deadlines looked up, open points named. What a tool cannot source does not go into a product.

## What we do not do

We do not advise and we do not represent. The products are guides, not legally binding documents, and do not replace an assessment of your individual case. No tool determines whether a rule applies to a specific business, and none rates individual vendors.

## Who is behind it

Scutoria is a small company based in Austria. Provider details are in the legal notice. Questions about access, invoices or content reach us at kontakt@scutoria.com and are answered within two working days.

Source-based. No legal advice. "Check the status" means: the date and the source are given; the assessment of your case stays yours.
