# Privacy notice

## Controller

Stefan Dallinger, Kleinpoppen 18, 3902 Vitis, Austria, kontakt@scutoria.com. Provider details are in the legal notice.

## Principle

This website itself sets one cookie only: the session cookie of your customer account, only when you sign in and only for as long as you stay signed in (thirty days at most). It is strictly necessary for signing in and therefore needs no consent (section 165 (3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021). In addition, the infrastructure of our host, Cloudflare, Inc., sets the cookie “__cf_bm” when you visit, to recognise and block automated access. According to Cloudflare it contains encrypted information for bot detection, is not linked to any identifier in our website and expires after 30 minutes without activity (Cloudflare, “Cloudflare Cookies”, as of 12 October 2026, retrieved 2 November 2026). It protects the service and needs no consent for the same reason. The website uses no third-party advertising or analytics services and loads no fonts or scripts from other servers. Which service providers receive data is listed in full in the sections below.

## Hosting, database and server logs

The website is run and delivered on the Lovable platform; the contracting party is Lovable Labs Incorporated, 1 Lincoln St, Boston, MA 02111, USA. The website is delivered through Cloudflare, Inc., which Lovable uses for routing, DNS and running the website. Your data from the account, the generator, the waiting list and memo requests is held in a Lovable Cloud database operated with Supabase, Inc., in an Amazon Web Services data centre in Ireland (region eu-west-1). When you visit, these providers process technically necessary data (IP address, time, requested address, browser identifier) in logs to provide and secure the service. Under its data processing agreement Lovable is itself responsible for these logs; Lovable states no fixed retention period, the logs are kept for as long as needed to run and secure the service. Legal basis: legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR) and, for account and purchase, performance of the contract (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). Data processing agreement with Lovable: agreement under Art. 28 GDPR, included in the Lovable Business plan that Scutoria uses. Lovable is a company in the USA; where data reaches the USA or other countries outside the EEA, this happens on the basis of the EU standard contractual clauses, which Lovable incorporates in its data processing agreement.

## Protection against abuse

So that forms cannot be used in bulk, we count requests per email address, for example at most three memo emails per address and hour. For this we do not store the address in clear text, only as a SHA-256 hash with the time. Entries older than 24 hours are deleted on later requests. Legal basis: legitimate interest in protecting the service (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

## Reach measurement without personal data

We count page views per day, page, language and referring domain. No IP address, browser identifier or cookie is stored; the figures cannot be linked to a person.

## Sending email and the mail log

We send email through Brevo (Brevo GmbH, Köpenicker Straße 126, 10179 Berlin, Germany, a company of Brevo SAS, Paris) as a processor (Art. 28 GDPR). For every email the website sends (sign-in link, access after purchase, memo, reminder before the licence ends, notification) we keep a log entry with the recipient address, subject, time and result; if an email could not be delivered, also its text, so that we can fix the fault. We delete the log after twelve months. Legal basis: legitimate interest in verifiable and reliable delivery (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

## Notification about the next toolkit

If you sign up for the notification, we store your email address, the chosen language and the time of your consent in order to notify you once when the next toolkit is available. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by email to kontakt@scutoria.com. The address is deleted after the notification has been sent, at the latest twelve months after your consent.

## Memo generators

The four memo generators (NIS2, labelling under Article 50, sign-in security, where to start) take your answers through the address of the result page; nothing is stored for a memo that is only read. We ask for a business name only when you request the memo as a PDF by email; it appears only in the PDF and is not stored. If you ask for the memo by email, we store your email address, the memo requested, its result, the chosen language, whether you want the notification about the next toolkit, and the time of your request in order to send the memo once as a PDF, and we count the request without any personal data. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by email to kontakt@scutoria.com. The details are deleted 90 days after your request; if you also asked for the notification about the next toolkit, the previous section applies to that.

## Purchase, invoice and payment

When you buy, we process your email address, invoicing details (name, address, company and VAT number where applicable), the product, the price and the time, in order to perform the contract, provide access and the invoice, and meet legal duties (Art. 6 (1) (b) and (c) GDPR). Payment is processed by Stripe Payments Europe, Limited, 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Grand Canal Dock, Dublin, Ireland. You enter your payment details (card, bank account) directly with Stripe; we do not receive them. Stripe processes data on our behalf where it handles the payment for us, and as an independent controller for its own purposes, such as fraud prevention and statutory anti-money-laundering duties. Stripe's privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy. We keep invoicing data for seven years (section 132 of the Austrian Federal Fiscal Code).

## Account and licence

In the customer account we store your email address, your answers in the generator, the generated documents and the licence status in order to provide the service (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). The answers may contain names, phone numbers and addresses that you enter for your documents, for example the responsible person and the deputy; you decide what you enter. Answers and documents are kept for the licence term and twelve months after it; after that we delete them. Generator runs that belong to no account are deleted 90 days after their last change. You can have the account deleted at any time; invoicing data is retained because of the statutory duty. For signing in we set the session cookie named above; sign-in links are valid for thirty minutes (seven days from the welcome mail) and work once.

## Withdrawal through the website

If you withdraw from the contract through the “Withdraw from contract” function, we process your name, email address, the order details and the time in order to handle the withdrawal and confirm its receipt to you. Legal basis: performance of the contract and legal obligation (Art. 6 (1) (b) and (c) GDPR). The details are kept in the mail log and deleted with it after twelve months, unless they must be retained as part of the accounts.

## Email contact

If you write to us, we process your details to answer you. Legal basis: pre-contractual steps or legitimate interest in answering (Art. 6 (1) (b) and (f) GDPR). Deleted once settled, unless a retention duty applies.

## Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Contact kontakt@scutoria.com. Complaints can be lodged with the Austrian Data Protection Authority: Barichgasse 40-42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at.
