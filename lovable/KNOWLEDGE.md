Scutoria sells dated document toolkits to EU businesses with no IT department, up to about fifty staff. English and German are equal: English at /, German under /de/. The brand has no face, no founder story, no consulting, no chat.

This project is a faithful rebuild of an approved site (reference: https://scutoria-site.onrender.com). Texts and design are final. Never rewrite, shorten, translate or add copy. Never restyle.

Voice on the site: "Sie". Voice inside the documents: "du". Short sentences, concrete things, numbers often written out. No emoji. No em dash as an aside. No exclamation marks.

Never write: compliant, certified, secure, safe, "meets all requirements", "GDPR ready", "AI Act ready". Never hide an open point. A complete answer may be "not yet, with a date". Not legal advice. No customer logos, testimonials, star ratings, or invented counts.

Design: the site is dark (night archive), documents are light paper; the only light is paper. Use the existing stylesheets tokens.css, bundle.css and site.css with their class names; no Tailwind or shadcn styling on these pages; no glassmorphism, neon, gradient mesh, custom cursor, countdown, confetti or chatbot. Type: Manrope for headings and text, IBM Plex Mono for kickers, sources, markers, prices, document numbers; both self-hosted, no third font, no Google Fonts.

Accent blue is for the one primary action and for links. Each product line has its own colour (policy blue, security teal, setup amber). Four markers, always with their shape, never colour alone: fact square, interpretation diamond, uncertain half-circle, gap dashed circle. Violet belongs only to the interpretation marker. Status lamps: green, amber, red.

Motion is short and once: hero sheet and cover settle on load, the marker shapes draw once, linked paper lifts 8px on hover and focus, the cover turns a little on hover. Reduced motion shows final states at once.

No third-party script, no analytics, no tracker, no cookie banner, no external font. Only the account's session cookie. Page views counted without personal data.

Keep every URL path of the reference site. Keep preview mode: noindex on every page and the preview chip until the owner says otherwise. Do not publish without the owner's explicit word.

Sign-in only by email link, no passwords. The generator never asks for passwords or staff names. Secrets (Stripe, Brevo) only through the secret form, never in code or chat.

Every number, deadline and legal statement in the content carries its source and retrieval date; do not change them.
