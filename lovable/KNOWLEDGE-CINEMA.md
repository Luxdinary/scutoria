Scutoria sells dated document toolkits to EU businesses with no IT department, up to about fifty staff. English and German are equal: English at /, German under /de/. The brand has no face, no founder story, no consulting, no chat.

This project runs the approved site (server-rendered pages in src/legacy, served through src/routes/$.tsx and src/lib/legacy-handler.ts). Texts are final: never rewrite, shorten, translate or add copy. Voice on the site: "Sie". No emoji. No em dash as an aside. No exclamation marks. Never write: compliant, certified, secure, safe, "meets all requirements", "GDPR ready", "AI Act ready", "verified". Never hide an open point. No customer logos, testimonials, star ratings, invented counts, seals or percentages.

Design since 3 November 2026: Sapphire Cinema (the owner's choice). The frame is deep navy with sapphire accents; the stylesheets are public/tokens.css (generated from the owner's design tokens; do not edit by hand), public/bundle.css, public/site.css, public/motion.css and public/cinema.css, loaded in that order by src/legacy/layout.mjs. The owner's Claude Code session authors the design files and sends them as complete files; apply them byte for byte, keep every class name, and do not restyle with Tailwind or shadcn. No Google Fonts, no third-party script, no cookie, no tracker. CSP stays default-src 'self' with no inline styles. Type: Manrope and IBM Plex Mono, self-hosted.

Four markers, always with their shape: fact square, interpretation diamond, uncertain half-circle, gap dashed circle. Accent blue for links, icons, focus and active states; the button token for the one primary action. Each product line has its own colour (policy blue, security teal, setup amber). The hero image /img/studio-stage-*.webp is a symbolic image and makes no claim about storage or security.

Pages: home, library (/library, /de/bibliothek), product pages, tools and memo generators, studio (/studio, /de/studio), handbooks, account, checkout, legal pages. New pages are mounted from src/legacy/server.mjs; the catch-all route needs no change. Content for the studio page lives in content/studio.json; nothing there is published yet and the page says so.

Rules for every change: keep tools, data processing, checkout, account, mail and legal behaviour unchanged; run the test suite and update tests that assert the old home markup (home-e29, header-csp-404, phase4-axe, transitions, app-routing) to the new markup and routes; never deploy. Publishing happens only on the owner's explicit word.
