Part II · Section 7

What belongs in a usage policy

FactArticle 4 of the AI Act requires providers and deployers to take measures to ensure a sufficient level of AI literacy among their staff and other persons operating AI systems on their behalf.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Art. 4 · EUR-Lex · 13 June 2024 · retrieved 2026-10-18

InterpretationA policy that explains the regulation is not read. Write the nine decisions the management has taken, one paragraph each, and put the reasoning in this handbook instead.

UncertainIs a written policy legally required?
No explicit duty is found in the AI Act or the GDPR. Supervisory guidance recommends documented internal instructions.
